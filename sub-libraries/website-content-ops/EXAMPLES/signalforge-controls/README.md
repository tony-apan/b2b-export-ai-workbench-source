# SignalForge Controls Buyer-Visible Positive Draft Fixture

This directory contains one **fully synthetic, copyable buyer-visible Draft fixture**:

- [B2B technical-readiness article structure](b2b-seo-positive-structure-article.md)

Use it to study or copy the **buyer-visible article body shape**: a direct opening, fit and exclusion boundaries, pain-to-decision flow, decision blocks, one worksheet, a bounded solution-family link, separated review gates and tiered CTAs. The `page_h1` frontmatter belongs to the page shell; the content between the unique `PUBLISHABLE_BODY_START` and `PUBLISHABLE_BODY_END` markers starts at H2 and does not repeat the H1.

This fixture is **not a complete four-record article package**. It does not replace an Article Brief, Article Quality Review or Publish Record, and it must not be passed to the four-record package validator as if those records existed.

SignalForge Controls, its people, product family, URLs, worksheet, route and workflow are fictional. This fixture demonstrates structure only. It is **not** a real customer record, CMS record, site, endpoint, renderer result, production evidence, search-demand validation, SEO result, ranking result, inquiry result, conversion result or revenue proof.
