## Clearance Boundary

- `method-use clearance` only authorizes the stated internal research or transformation method; it does not authorize publication.
- `redaction_status: safe-to-publish` is a content-safety statement, not publication clearance.
- `publication clearance` remains `pending` or `blocked` until a named owner, decision date, scope, and evidence are recorded.
- A source with `pending`, `blocked`, `unknown`, or missing publication clearance cannot support a release or Public Preview PASS.
